## `⌨` About

I explore the intersection of **artificial intelligence, technology, content creation and automation**. My focus is practical: understand what a tool does, where it is useful, what its limitations are, and how people can apply it in real workflows.

- **AI & prompt engineering** — models, tools, prompting methods and practical use cases.
- **Technology explainers** — breaking down technical concepts into clear, accessible language.
- **Creator workflows** — AI-assisted production, automation and repeatable content systems.
- **Hardware & future tech** — PCs, benchmarking, automotive innovation and emerging technology.
- **Malayalam tech education** — making useful technology knowledge accessible to curious Malayali audiences.
